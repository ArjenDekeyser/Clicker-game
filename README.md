# Moon Orchard: Cosmic Clicker

An original, multi-world idle clicker. Harvest resources, grow unusual gardens, automate production, and explore three distinct worlds.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Keep `world-packs.js` beside it; that file contains the Abyssal Archive and Clockwork Wilds content. Progress saves automatically in this browser, with up to eight hours of offline harvest.

## Progression

- Click the current world’s moon, bubble, or gear to collect its resource.
- Buy upgrades for click power, autoclicking, producers, and production boosts. Use the shop filters and 1, 10, 100, or MAX purchase modes.
- Unlock gardens for stronger production bonuses.
- Complete eight milestones and eight expeditions in each world to open the World Gate. Entering a new world resets that world’s run and loads a new setting, click target, currency, gardens, and upgrade collection. Genesis Seeds and worlds remade carry over.
- Cosmic Rebirth resets current resources, upgrades, and garden unlocks in exchange for that world’s permanent production tokens.
- World Reforge unlocks at 1 septillion world harvest. It resets the current world’s progress and awards Genesis Seeds, each giving a permanent 1.25× production multiplier.
- Complete expeditions to earn Star Shards for permanent constellation upgrades, and catch wandering comets for bonus harvest and a temporary production boost.

## Worlds

1. **Moon Orchard** — Stardust in a lunar garden.
2. **Abyssal Archive** — Pearls in an underwater reef.
3. **Clockwork Wilds** — Gears in an enchanted mechanical forest.

## Other features

- Developer controls from the moon button (code: `jens is de beste`)
- Sound toggle, reset confirmation, responsive layout, and local browser saves
