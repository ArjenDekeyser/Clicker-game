# Moon Orchard

Moon Orchard is an original cosmic idle clicker. Click the moon to gather stardust, build an automated lunar orchard, and grow your harvest across distant gardens.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No install or server is required. Your progress saves automatically in that browser on your device.

## How to play

- Click the large moon to collect stardust. Your click upgrades increase each harvest.
- Buy upgrades in the Orchard Shop. Producers earn stardust over time; autoclickers add clicks automatically.
- Choose 1, 10, 100, or MAX to buy upgrades in bulk as prices grow.
- Use the shop filters to browse click upgrades, autoclickers, producers, and global boosts.
- Unlock new gardens with stardust. Your active garden boosts all production, and later gardens offer stronger bonuses.
- Complete world-harvest milestones to earn bonus stardust. Milestone progress and rewards persist through Cosmic Rebirth, but reset when you reforge the world.
- Complete garden expeditions to earn Star Shards, then spend them on permanent constellation upgrades.
- Catch a wandering comet for a burst of stardust and a short production boost.
- After gathering enough world stardust, choose **Cosmic Rebirth**. This resets your current stardust, upgrades, and garden unlocks in exchange for Moonstones. Each Moonstone permanently increases production by 10%.
- At 1 septillion world stardust, choose **World Reforge** for a true reset: harvest, upgrades, gardens, Moonstones, milestones, expeditions, and constellations all restart. Earn Genesis Seeds for a permanent 1.25× production multiplier each; Seeds and worlds remade persist.
- Complete the orchard by reaching 10²⁸ world stardust, unlocking every garden, and owning an Omniverse Orchard to open **The Reflection Sea**. Its Echoes and upgrades are separate from Stardust and persist through World Reforge.
- In the Reflection Sea, the tide alternates every 25 seconds: **Stillness** doubles passive Echo Wells, while **Fracture** quadruples tap power. Shift polarity manually when timing matters, then build a separate Echo upgrade collection.

## Features

- 90 Stardust upgrades spanning click power, autoclicking, production, and global boosts, balanced around consistent payback windows by tier
- Nine separate Echo upgrades in the post-game Reflection Sea
- Nine unlockable lunar gardens with increasing production bonuses
- 17 world-harvest milestones and 14 garden expeditions for long-run goals
- Three permanent constellation paths
- Wandering comet events with bonus stardust and temporary production boosts
- Automatic and offline harvest (offline earnings are capped at eight hours)
- Rebirth progression with permanent production bonuses
- A full world-ascension reset with permanent Genesis Seed progression
- Responsive layout, sound toggle, field notes, and reset confirmation
- Local browser save; no account or server required
